About Aurova
Handmade

Aurova Handmade is a name born from Aurora and Nova, bringing together the beauty of light, colour and wonder with the idea of new beginnings and something that shines.

Just like our name, we believe every handmade creation should carry its own little spark. Through crochet, we turn simple yarn into thoughtful pieces filled with creativity, warmth and personality.
